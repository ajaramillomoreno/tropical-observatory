Welcome to the Tropical Weather & Climate Observatory

The Tropical Weather & Climate Observatory is a personal scientific project born out of curiosity, a love of atmospheric science, and a fascination with the tropics. It is a place to explore weather and climate, experiment with observational data, write code, learn new things, and simply enjoy the science. The observatory brings together maps and other scientific products that I develop along the way, sharing some of the beauty, complexity, and surprises of the tropical atmosphere.
